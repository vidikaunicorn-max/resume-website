Founder, Developer and Designer – Visionless Venture

2024 - Present

A pair of accessible, voice-first apps for blind and visually impaired users, built on Gemini AI. Awarded the CREST Gold Award by the British Science Association.

What I did

  • Built a transit app for the blind that gives real-time platform numbers and arrival times from spoken travel details, with voice-recorded requests, audio responses and directional guidance from signs to the correct platform inside the station
  • Built a fashion app that analyses outfit selfies and gives detailed audio feedback on style coordination, and suggests personalised outfits from photos of the user’s closet, with the exact location of each item
  • Designed custom Gemini-based workflows for image recognition, voice input and context-aware feedback across both apps
